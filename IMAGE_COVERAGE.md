# Image coverage map

Every product/context image in this repository appears in the finished reel.
The two logo files are excluded by design (Section 4a) and are not part of the
compulsory-coverage set.

- **Total enumerated images:** 68
- **Primary / hero tier** (composed inside the 250-1580px safe zone): 58
- **Ambient tier** (blurred fill in the top/bottom bands): 10
- **Runtime:** 2640 frames = 88.000s

> The stated count for this project was 73. A full `git ls-files` enumeration
> finds 68 non-logo images (70 image files minus the 2 logos); 5 of those are
> byte-identical duplicates of another file, so there are 63 unique pictures.
> All 68 files are placed regardless.
